## 📖 About the Project

**Nihongo with Nikki** is an immersive, gamified web application designed to make learning Japanese engaging and structured. Whether you are a beginner starting with Hiragana or an advanced learner tackling N1 Kanji, this dashboard provides all the tools you need in one place.

The interface features a beautiful dark mode UI, a virtual study companion (Nikki), and a comprehensive tracking system to keep you motivated on your language learning journey.

## ✨ Features

*   **📊 Comprehensive Dashboard:** Track your total XP, daily streaks, and overall progress toward your goals.
*   **📚 Structured Learning Modules:**
    *   **Kanji:** Master readings and meanings (N5 to N1).
    *   **Kana:** Perfect your Hiragana and Katakana.
    *   **Vocabulary:** Build your word bank by theme.
    *   **Grammar:** Learn essential grammar points.
    *   **Reading & Listening:** Practice with tailored passages and audio.
    *   **Stories:** Immersive reading practice written by Nikki.
*   **🎯 Daily Goals & Streaks:** Stay consistent with daily XP goals and streak tracking.
*   **🎮 Gamification:** Earn XP, level up, and get quick tips from your study companion.
*   **🌙 Modern UI/UX:** A sleek, anime-inspired dark mode interface built for focus and comfort.
